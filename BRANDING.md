# Branding assets

The PNGs under `custom_components/aosmith_ble/brand/` are unchanged A. O. Smith
assets from the Home Assistant brands repository, `core_integrations/aosmith`:
https://github.com/home-assistant/brands/tree/master/core_integrations/aosmith

A. O. Smith branding belongs to its respective owner and identifies the supported
manufacturer. This is an independent community integration, not an official
A. O. Smith product. The images are bundled according to Home Assistant's local
custom-integration brand mechanism (2026.3+):
https://developers.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/02/24/brands-proxy-api/

HACS detail pages render the README logo. Downloads-list support is tracked at:
https://github.com/hacs/integration/issues/5223
